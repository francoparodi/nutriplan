# Patient guide

## What is Nutriplan
Nutriplan is your **food diary**: here you find the meal plan prepared by your doctor, record day by day what you ate and log your weight. Your doctor can see how you are doing and step in when needed.

The foods in the plan are decided by your doctor: you can confirm your meals, use the **substitutions** they have allowed, enter the **quantity you actually ate** and note when you ate something different. You do not have to fill everything in every day, but the more complete your diary is, the better your doctor can help you.


## First login
1. Open Nutriplan and enter your **username** and the **temporary password** given by your doctor.
2. You will be asked to choose a **new personal password** (at least 8 characters). The temporary one works only once.
3. You stay logged in even if you close the browser, until you press **Log out**.

## Daily diary
The left-hand menu opens and closes with the button at the top.

- **Diary** shows the day with the meals in your diet. Use the arrows or the calendar to go **back in time** and fill in past days too.
- For each meal choose:
  - **As planned**: if there are several alternatives (A, B, C…) tap the one you followed, then **Save**.
  - **I ate something else**: describe what you ate instead. Your doctor will see it.
  - **Skipped**: the meal was not eaten.
- If you ate a different amount than planned, correct it in the field next to the food: the value is recorded and your doctor sees it **highlighted** as "quantity changed". Foods cannot be changed, except with the allowed substitutions.

### Substitutions
Next to foods marked with **\*** there is a **Substitutions** button: it lists only the equivalents allowed by your doctor, already scaled to your quantity. Pick one and save the meal.

### Weekly limits
Some items (dessert, pizza, pesto…) have a weekly maximum: you can see how many times you have used them.

### Warnings
If some days have unconfirmed meals an orange warning lists them: tap a day to complete it. You are not obliged to fill in every day, but your doctor sees what is missing.

## Allergies, intolerances and conditions
In **Profile**, in the "Allergies, intolerances and conditions" section, you can report what your doctor must keep in mind. They will see it and get a warning every time they prepare or edit your diet.

## My visits
In **Profile**, below your details, you find the visits recorded by your doctor: measurements (waist, calf, biceps…), notes and attachments. They are read-only.

## Weight
In **Weight** enter your weight with the date (past days too) and see the chart and table. You can also enter it from the diary.

## My diet
Shows the full plan, substitution groups and general guidelines. **Download PDF** to keep it with you.

## Progress and reports
The **Progress** page shows weight and adherence over time. At the bottom, choose a date range and download the report as **PDF** or **Excel**.

## Documents
Recipes and documents shared with you by your doctor. You can also **upload** test results, analyses or photos: your doctor will see them in your record. You can delete only the files you uploaded.

## Profile, theme and language
In **Profile** you can optionally enter your **contacts** (phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, website) and see your doctor's. You can also add or change your **photo** (visible to you and your doctor) and change your password. Light/dark theme, IT/EN language and sidebar open/closed are in the left menu and are remembered in the browser.

## Forgot your password
Contact your doctor: they will give you a new one-time temporary password to replace at login.
